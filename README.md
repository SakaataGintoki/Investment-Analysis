# Investment-Analysis
# Investment Analysis Overview
This analysis provides a comprehensive view of investment behaviors based on profession, gender, age, and financial goals. 
Professionals invest significantly more than non-working individuals, particularly in bonds, land, and fixed deposits. Males dominate overall investment and income, especially in the 18–27 age group, which contributes the majority of total investments. 
Common investment goals include financial freedom, tax savings, and wealth generation, with family/friends and mobile apps being the most used resources for making investment decisions.

# Age and Gender wise Metrics
Description:
Focuses on investment distribution by age and gender, showing total contributions and investment behaviors across age groups.

Key Insights:
Males invest far more (₹1,73,89,200) than females (₹66,42,000).
The 18-27 age group contributes the most to total investments (₹1,63,93,200, 68%).
Within age groups, males consistently outperform females in investment amount.
Investments sharply decline after age 37, indicating early-stage investment dominance.

# Investment & Income Metrics
Description:
This section analyzes investment patterns across professions and genders. It includes total investments, average monthly contributions, years of investing, and income distribution.

Key Insights:
Professionals invest significantly more in bonds, lands, and FDs compared to non-working individuals.
Bonds and lands are the most common long-term investments with average holding periods of 10 and 9 years respectively.
Males earn a higher average income (₹1,85,620) than females (₹1,37,500).
Digital currency and advertising are relatively new investment areas with lower average years invested.

# Goal Metrics
Description:
This dashboard evaluates how working status influences investment goals and the resources used for decision-making.

Key Insights:
Professionals invest more heavily in financial freedom, wealth generation, and tax savings.
Non-working individuals prioritize personal savings and marriage as key goals.
Family/friends and mobile applications are the top sources used for investment decisions.
